# User guide

Everyone logs in at **/login** with their email and password. The left menu only shows what your role can use.

## Guests
**Book a room.** Home page → choose dates and guests on the key card → *Check availability* → open a room → *Confirm booking*. You need an account (*Create an account* takes a minute). The booking code appears in *My bookings*.

**Change or cancel.** Open the booking → *Cancel booking* (possible until the day before arrival; same-day changes go through the front desk).

**During your stay.** *Overview* shows your room, key card and check-out time.
- *Requests*: pick a service (room service, laundry, wake-up call, transport, spa) and send it. You can follow its status and cancel it while it is still new.
- *Report a problem*: tell us what is broken in your room.
- *See my bill*: open the booking to see the running total.

**After your stay.** *My invoices* → open an invoice to print or save it as PDF. *Feedback* → rate the stay.

**Privacy.** *My profile* → download all your data, turn marketing emails on or off, or close your account.

## Receptionist
- **Front desk** is your home screen: arrivals (*Check in*), departures (*Check out*) and guests in house.
- **Walk-in or phone booking**: *Front desk → Walk-in booking* or *Reservations → New reservation*. Search the guest (or create a profile), choose dates, *Find free rooms*, pick one, confirm.
- **Check in** issues a key card number and marks the room occupied. If the room is still being cleaned, change the booking to another room first (*Change*).
- **Check out** shows the bill. Add a discount if needed, take payment (or leave 0 to bill later) and confirm. The invoice opens and housekeeping is told to clean the room.
- **Extra charges** (mini bar, late check-out): open the booking → *Add charge*.
- **Payments later**: *Invoices* → open the invoice → *Record a payment*.
- **Guest services**: start and complete guest requests; adjust the charge if needed.

## Housekeeping
- **Housekeeping** page: *To do*, *In progress*, *Done*. Press *Start* to claim a task, *Done* when finished. Finishing a check-out clean makes the room available to sell.
- **Room board**: set a room to cleaning, available or maintenance.
- **Report issue**: anything broken goes straight to the managers.

## Manager
Everything the receptionist can do, plus:
- **Room board**: add rooms, edit rates and photos, retire rooms.
- **Maintenance**: update status, add what was done. Resolving an issue on a blocked room sends it to housekeeping for inspection.
- **Feedback**: reply to guests and choose which reviews appear on the website.
- **Reports**: occupancy, revenue, average daily rate, RevPAR, ratings, a 30-night forecast and pricing advice. *Export CSV* downloads the daily numbers.

## Administrator
Everything above, plus:
- **Staff & roles**: create staff accounts (they get an email to set their own password), change roles, send password reset emails, deactivate people who leave, and see the permission matrix.
- **Settings**: hotel details, currency, tax rate, check-in/out times, standard room rates (*Save and apply to all rooms*), service prices and policies.
- **Reports → Pricing suggestions → Apply** updates a room type's rate in one step.

## FAQ
**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in page choose *Forgot password?*, enter your email and open the link we send (valid 30 minutes). Staff can also ask an administrator to send a reset email from *Staff & roles → Edit*.
**How do I change my password?** *My profile → Password & security → Email me a reset link*.
**I am new staff. What is my password?** You choose it yourself: open the "Set up your account" email and press *Set my password*.
**Why can't I check a guest in?** Check-in opens on the arrival date, and the room must be free and clean.
**Why is a room missing from the search?** It is booked for those dates, under maintenance, or too small for the number of guests.
**Do old bookings change when I change a rate?** No. Bookings keep the rate they were made at.
